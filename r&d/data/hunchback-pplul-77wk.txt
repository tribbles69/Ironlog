 2. Fitting 12 Exercises Into 45 Minutes
0:00-0:08          Warm-up: cat-camel x8 · dead bug x6/side · band pull-apart x15 · then ramp the main lift (bar x8 → 50% x5 → 65% x3 → 78% x2
                   → 88% x1)

0:08-0:28          Main compound: top set + back-offs. 2-3 min rest between heavy sets. This is the session - it never gets cut.

0:28-0:42          Supersets (marked SS): alternate the pair back to back, ~60 s between rounds. Two supersets = four exercises in ~14 min. This is
                   the whole trick.

0:42-0:45          Finisher: calves or abs, straight sets, 45 s rest. Walk out.
 The triage order when time runs out (it will, some weeks): drop the finisher, then the second superset, then the first. Main lift
 never. A 25-minute session that hit the top set is a successful session; a 45-minute session that skipped it isn't.


 3. Progression, Deloads & Safety (from the 24-week doc, kept intact)
The RIR rule                +2.5 kg next exposure when every set hit with 1+ RIR. Repeat the weight if you grind to 0 RIR - earn it clean before you
                            jump. Drop 5% and rebuild after two consecutive misses on the same lift. On B weeks, progress by reps first (top of the
                            range), then load.

The AMRAP engine            AMRAP = as many clean reps as possible, stop 1 shy of failure. It does two jobs: at equipment caps (calf raise 225, leg
                            extension 102.5, hip ab/ad 80, chest press 110, cable crunch 45, face pull 45, hip thrust bar-capped 220) reps replace
                            kilos - that IS the progression. On last back-off sets it auto-progresses: beat target by 3+ reps → next A-week's guide
                            goes up 2.5 kg beyond what's printed.

Guides, not scripture       Printed loads are RPE-anchored guides (RPE 8 volume blocks, 9 strength, 9.5 peak). Moving better? +2.5 kg on the spot.
                            Top set turns into a grinder? Back-offs drop 5 kg, next A-week repeats. The bar outranks the paper, in both directions.

Deloads                     Built into the calendar: wk 8, wk 12, Christmas (wk 24), wk 32, wks 40-41, wks 47 & 53, wk 57, wk 69, wks 74-77. Half the
                            sets, ~60% loads. Re-test e1RM at the Bridge (wks 15-16) and recalibrate at wk 64 - nowhere else.

Peaking & attempts          Rehearse full commands from wk 33 (squat depth+rack, bench start/press/rack, deadlift down). Openers should feel like
                            a confident double's in the tank - bank the total, then push. Last truly heavy session: wk 37 (Spring), wk 72 (Winter). After
                            that it's taper - resist testing. Pick seconds and thirds from how the singles actually moved, not from this sheet.

Stop immediately if         Dizziness or light-headedness, chest tightness or pain, or any visual disturbance under load. Given the kyphosis: stop
                            the set the moment bracing fails or the thoracic curve collapses under the bar. A missed rep is cheap; a positional grind is
                            not. No passive stretching, ever - Beighton 8/9 controls range, it doesn't chase it.

Staying 93 kg               Fasted morning weigh-in, act on the 7-day average only. Bulk ceiling 94.5 • prep corridor 92.5-93.5 • wake-up 92.0-92.5
                            from 10 days out (carb/salt trim only - never water cuts, not with your job's sweat load). Calories: bulk ~3,800-4,000 • cut
                            wks 9-13 ~3,200-3,400 + 180 g protein • preps ~3,600-3,800 • hypertrophy ~3,900-4,000.
